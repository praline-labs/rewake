# Stage 3 of 2.0: S18 in progress

S18 is the eighteenth step of stage 3 ([stage3.md](../v2/stage3.md)): the decision that
disposes an unknown stop and the recovery that installs its effects
([stage3-decision.md](../v2/stage3-decision.md),
[stage3-decision-recovery.md](../v2/stage3-decision-recovery.md)). The Codex side's
readiness review asked fourteen questions; main adopted the answers Q1–Q14 on October 12,
2026, recorded in [stage3-decision-build.md](../v2/stage3-decision-build.md), and split the
step into three parts, each reviewed and accepted on the Codex side before the next starts:
records, readers and recovery with no public writer; authority and host lifetime; the
`decide` command, its views and the end-to-end gate. S18 is built on branch `s3-s18` from
`3ea7870`. This entry records part 1, built on October 12, 2026, not accepted at its first
review the same day, corrected, and accepted on the Codex side at `d025257` the same day, and
part 2, built, not accepted at its first review, corrected and accepted on the Codex side at
`7deee6e` the same day. Part 3 is to come.

## Part 1: records, readers and recovery

- **The documents** (`e9471bb`): Q1–Q14 as adopted, the world and crash manifest
  ([stage3-decision-manifest.md](../v2/stage3-decision-manifest.md)) with a model package
  that enumerates it and a test that checks its counts, part B of the plan corrected to the
  current tree.
- **The records** (`9823738`, `583124c`): strict readers of the decision, its closure, a
  note's told record and a resend's summary; a read capture of what the decision saw; four
  new record kinds; a read-only view of the decisions; a disposal that drops exactly the
  occurrence a decision names; retention keeps every `decided-*` journal.
- **The recovery** (`fd2afa4`, `93af6b4`, `7ab4c53`): the installation runs before the stop
  in the barrier — the resend `decided-<id>` linked once, the notes published through the
  report's section with a told record; the delivery of a report reads the decision before
  any other evidence; the closure is written after the tail and before the journal is done.
  The plan runs the installation read-only; a plan scene, a write-cut test of the
  installation and an oracle test of one and two generations against the model.

Mutants: 30 for the records and 25 for the recovery, each killed by the test it was
written for. No production code writes a decision; the tests write one under a real build

## Review: ten findings and two gaps

The review on the Codex side, at `7ab4c53`, did not accept part 1. The fixes, each with a
regression red before and green after and a mutant:

- **R1** (`bd44253`, `3a49dda`, `ed5e53c`): recovery acted on a decision whose link was
  visible while its directory flush had failed. The installation now flushes the decision,
  its directory and the mailbox before any effect and writes a `.durable` marker held to the
  decision; disposal and the evidence of an installation act only on a durable decision.
  The state seam gained a flush fault and a file sync of regular files alone.
- **R2** (`a746c5a`): the installation, now before the scanner, followed a link or blocked
  on a FIFO in a decision's place. Every mailbox record is read through a bounded,
  no-follow, nonblocking open of regular files; the decisions directory is checked with
  `lstat`.
- **R3** (`5529efa`): a saved decided step could stand without its decision. It requires its
  decision, about a report the journal carries, about the journal's operation.
- **R4** (`c5e710d`, `1e5f8b9`): a resend of another kind was completed and lost. A
  `decided-*` journal is read by its own strict envelope in both forms and held to its
  decision.
- **R5** (`e5c3ea8`): another note's told record suppressed this note. A told record is held
  to its note, name and run.
- **R6** (`4d1fb75`): unknown liveness became a permanent run-ended answer. Only a proven end
  is told so; anything else leaves the note owed.
- **R7** (`414f8e7`): a closure of another operation or another run's waits was accepted. It
  is held to its journal's operation and exactly the tail's waits.
- **R8** (`e7ff826`): an empty listing did not survive the decision's encoding, and returned
  observations shared memory with the capture.
- **R9** (`e7ff826`): the strict reader accepted a stray closing delimiter after the value.
- **R10** (`c5e710d`): a resend summary that did not read destroyed a readable done
  envelope. It is reported apart, the completion kept.
- **G1** (`48d50bb`): the manifest conflated a run's life at the decision with its later
  life. The partition is split by who decides, the run at the decision and the run after
  it; named schedules are tested both ways; the counts are 3481 feasible worlds and a cover
  of 108.
- **G2**: the typed inventory of stop readers and journal writers was taken with `go/types`
  over every build variant: every production reader of the stop goes through
  `world.stopState`, five production journal writers, the view of the decisions with no
  production caller yet; the consumers deferred to parts 2 and 3 named with their boundary.

`1e5f8b9` closed three gaps the mutants found, `d025257` dropped test parameters the lint
flagged. The review's own twelve probes pass on the final code; 30 mutants of the fix round

## Part 2: authority and host lifetime

Main confirmed on October 12, 2026 that part 2 builds the decision transaction itself as an
internal mail API, called by the verified wrapper and by the person's path, with no command.

- **The strict inventory** (`dac0cba`): `registry.Inventory` reads every record of a room as
  a regular file, never through a link, bounded, and keeps a run whose life cannot be read
  apart from one that ended.
- **The transaction** (`e905f06`): `mail.Decide` takes the sender's mailbox with a bounded
  wait, holds the subject to what the asker saw, refuses on proof found since the stop,
  freezes the notes owed from the room's main, writes the decision through the state seam
  and runs the installation and the barrier. A verified decider must be the recipient run
  and the writing process itself.
- **The person** (`68e686d`): a strict scan of every room of the selected root and of the
  real ancestor chain, read twice around one inventory and compared, at most three
  attempts. Main decided on October 12, 2026 that an ancestor of another user is read for
  its uid alone; the same holds for its namespaces.
- **The authority** (`042751f`): every wrapper, whatever its role, listens for decisions;
  only main's listener takes grants. The wrapper checks the asking peer's uid, namespaces
  and descent; the asker checks the listener's peer against the recipient run. At most four
  decisions at once, none once the run closes; a question that went out with no answer
  back is uncertain, never unreachable. Each admitted decision keeps the run's lease until
  it finishes.
- **The process rig** (`dc4afa5`): real fixture sessions and askers as separate processes,
  paused at named fault steps — forged callers, a false listener, compaction, a dropped and
  reconnected capability set, a replaced run, the wrapper killed on either side of the
  commit, the harness ending mid-decision. It found that admission closed late, in a
  deferred call after teardown began; it now closes as soon as the harness ends.

Mutants: 32, each killed (`d0408e1` closed the three the first run left).

## Review of part 2: two findings

The review on the Codex side, at `d0408e1`, did not accept part 2. The fixes, each with a
regression red before and green after and a mutant:

- **R1** (`2d6abc2`): a report of another sender run was taken for the subject. The subject
  holds the report's sender run to the journal's.
- **R2** (`6754370`, `7deee6e`): a dangling `rooms` or `sessions` directory read as an empty
  inventory, so the person path found no sessions and the transaction found no main. The
  inventory is opened without following a link at either level, the second relative to the
  first; the mailbox's own listings are read the same way. Only a directory missing for
  real reads as empty.

The review's three probes pass on the final code; seven mutants of the fix round are
killed; the five checks are green and the workflow suite passes all 107 cases. The second
look at `7deee6e` accepted part 2 on October 12, 2026. It carried two points into part 3: a
working verified decision from a recipient whose shell cannot reach a unix socket, with a
regression, a connection failure never falling back to the person and any change of
transport reviewed again; and a mailbox directory itself replaced by a dangling link,
which reads its stops as absent and so refuses, in the world matrix.
