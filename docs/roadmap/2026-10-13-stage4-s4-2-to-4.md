# Stage 4 of 2.0: the space rules, the protocol, the record and the terminal

Steps S4-2, S4-3 and S4-4 of the first working space
([the steps](../v2/stage4-space-steps.md#the-build-steps)), built October 13, 2026 on stacked
branches `s4-2`, `s4-3` and `s4-4` by the write session, each reviewed on the Codex side, and
landed together on `v2` at `f045ab6` the same day, after the five checks ran green there.

## What each step built

- **S4-2, the rules and the protocol** (`fb6b0e4`, `fe7740b`). The rules test learned the SP
  group, with SP3, SP4 and SP9 stated where existing tests already hold them
  ([rules/space.md](../rules/space.md)). The mod's protocol is one JSON schema,
  `internal/adapter/claude/mod/protocol.schema.json`, from which `tools/protogen` generates the
  Go types of `modhost` and the TypeScript types of the mod; a drift test fails when either
  generated file differs from a fresh generation or the generator meets a word of the schema it
  does not read.
- **S4-3, the space record** (`a2257c8`, `5349634`). `internal/core/space` keeps one record per
  space under the room's lease, its states in order — `opening`, `open`, `closing`, then `kept`
  or `closed` — and the kept checkouts; `rewake space list` reads them. SP2 and SP7 entered the
  rules ([the record](../v2/stage4-space-record.md)). The same branch recorded what agent-deck
  teaches for stage 4 and the owner's answers on how a space is shown (`26c5d11`).
- **S4-4, the terminal** (`b8f79e2`). `internal/host/terminal` hosts a program on a
  pseudo-terminal opened with the standard library and reads its screen back as styled runs
  through `charmbracelet/x/vt`, the owner's exception to the dependency rule, imported by this
  package alone; what the library brings in was measured and recorded
  ([the terminal](../v2/stage4-space-terminal.md#what-it-brings-in)).

## What the reviews found and how it was fixed

Four blockers across the three steps — one each in S4-2 and S4-3, two in S4-4 — every one
confirmed by main with the reviewer's probe before the fix, and each fix landing with a
regression test:

- **S4-2: an endpoint reference could drift with the test green.** `protogen` took a `$ref`
  without the `#/$defs/` prefix, so a changed reference generated the same files. Fixed in
  `b3f6287`: a non-local reference and text after the schema are refused, and the steps document
  now lists what each handler owes the schema's choices.
- **S4-3: an absent checkout observation read as a proven absence.** A close without its
  checkout's state, or with a value the record did not know, took the checkout as gone. Fixed in
  `6617ae8`: a close states whether the checkout was found there, gone or unknown, and an
  unknown checkout is kept and reminded.
- **S4-4: two blockers.** A failed setup of the pseudo-terminal left its master descriptor to
  a finalizer, so under a descriptor limit a retry could not even open the master; fixed in
  `408468a`, every failed step closing what it opened. And a post of keys could reach the
  program in several write calls, splitting what the key surface sent as one; fixed in
  `f045ab6` by main's decision: a post is at most 4096 bytes, written by exactly one write(2),
  and a short write is reported as partial instead of being finished. The same commit bounds a
  terminal's grid in cells.

## What stays open

- A turn's start time the mod cannot give stays unknown, never the arrival time (S4-6), and
  each handler validates the combinations the generated types cannot rule out
  ([the handlers' list](../v2/stage4-space-steps.md#the-build-steps)).
- `ScreenAnswer` carries no terminal modes; S4-8 adds them, by main's decision. Whether to
  answer with the person's colors stays with S4-8, and synchronized output is not honored
  ([left to the steps after](../v2/stage4-space-terminal.md#left-to-the-steps-after)).
- Whether a worker is alive and how it ended is the space host's (S4-7); a program that
  ignores SIGHUP is S4-7's and S4-9's escalation, not the terminal's.
- The record's writes from main's wrapper — opening in S4-7, closing and the next start's
  closing of a dead main's records in S4-9 — and the reminder of kept checkouts.
