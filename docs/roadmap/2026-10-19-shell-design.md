# The shell: spike and design

October 19, 2026, by the owner's decision to replace the host mode with a shell in TypeScript on
OpenTUI and Solid ([the design](../v2/shell.md)). A spike ran Claude Code 2.1.302 and pi 1.2.0 in
two `EmbeddedTerminalRenderable` panes (`@opentui/core` 0.6.6, Bun 1.4.0) under a stand-in
terminal: Shift+Enter, paste, the wheel, clicks, drags, OSC 52 copy, title, resize, focus and zoom
worked; a Solid variant showed a modal over running panes and a compiled single binary. Open: the
owner's questions in the report, bell and notifications, the theme followed live, the person's own
terminal, then the steps.
