# Stage 3 of 2.0: S5 built and accepted

S5 is the fifth step of stage 3 ([stage3.md](../v2/stage3.md)): the neutral confirmation,
and the fixture harness beside the two real ones as the workflow suite's third column
([stage3-steps.md](../v2/stage3-steps.md#s5-the-confirmation-and-the-fixture-beside-the-others-codex),
[stage3-fixture.md](../v2/stage3-fixture.md)). It was built on October 7, 2026 in two
commits. Six fix commits followed, after three review rounds, and the Codex side
accepted the whole range `9e91db5..9ce148d` the same day.

## The two commits

1. `6b8dba7`: the neutral confirmation.
   - `CompletionHandler.Confirm` and the CLI shim `ConfirmCompletion` hold an end and
     answer with the reason the session continues with.
   - A held end is known by its operation, recorded in the kept answer and in the
     journal of the end that takes it. A repeated `Confirm` gets the same answer.
   - A recovered hold raises the read clock to its own position.
2. `730141b`: the fixture harness, behind the `rewakefixture` tag.
   - Its adapter, with the readiness exchange, and its program.
   - L1, L2 and E3 on its launch and turns.
   - `TestPendingConfirm` and `TestWithdrawMidTurn` on it.
   - The end-identity oracles, rebuilt on neutral completions.
   - The workflow suite, which had 163 cases on two columns, ran 189 on three: 183
     passed and 6 were unsupported, each for a named capability.

## The review, in three rounds

**Round 1** found eight defects; acceptance was withheld for both commits.

- O1 with several senders: a failed lookup of one sender still let a live one hold the
  end, where every failure of the hold check must fall through to publication.
- Four defects of the fixture:
  - an end captured its boundary when its work ran, not when its frame arrived;
  - `Close` returned while calls into the handler could still run;
  - a withdrawal did not stop an end's further attempts;
  - one silent probe failed the whole start rather than its own capability.
- The tagged test line had been narrowed to two packages around a layout failure. That
  workaround was refused. The rule itself was fixed instead: a harness built only for
  tests is found by its title in any text, and by its id only as a whole string literal.
  The whole tree's tests now run with the fixture in the catalog, alone and with the
  fault seam ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#checks)).
- O2 had no scenario on the fixture column for a failed or interrupted turn's reason.
- A mutant the first report called equivalent was observable.

**Round 2** closed seven of the eight. Two things stayed open:

- Finding 4 kept a window between the reader closing a connection and the withdrawal
  that follows it. A probe showed a second attempt in that window.
- The per-commit check logs had been taken on working trees that were not clean.

**Round 3** accepted the range, Codex side included.

## The fixes, as new commits

The rewrite of the branch that would have folded the fixes into the two commits was
refused by the session's permission check. Main decided against a rewrite: the fixes
land as new commits on top of `730141b`, each green, and review reads the range as it is.

1. `5d48130`: the hold check fails when a sender's record cannot be read, so the end is
   published to every sender (finding 1).
2. `25f89c3`: four changes to the fixture.
   - An end's boundary is captured when its frame arrives.
   - `Close` joins every call into the handler.
   - An end's attempts are bound to the connection it came on.
   - Each probe is bounded on its own.
3. `fb2c76f`: the full tagged runs and the name rule (finding 6). Three `internal/cli`
   tests that iterate every harness were fixed in their own setup; no product code
   changed.
4. `b461c92`: the regressions that the surviving mutants of round 1's fixes asked for.
5. `085857e`: the `turn-reasons` scenario on the fixture column, with three controls
   (finding 7).
6. `9ce148d`: `liveOn` refuses a closed connection itself, so no turn and no end attempt
   starts between the close and the withdrawal (round 2).

## The evidence

- **Checks.** The five checks were run again on a fresh detached worktree of each of
  the eight commits:
  - HEAD equal to the commit, and `git status` empty before and after;
  - each commit checked by the commands its own AGENTS.md states;
  - every exit code 0.

  The three commits before `fb2c76f` carry the narrowed tagged line of their time. From
  `fb2c76f` both full tagged runs apply. On `9ce148d`:

  | test run | pass | skip |
  |---|---|---|
  | untagged | 5196 | 202 |
  | each tagged run | 5259 | 202 |
- **The workflow suite after `9ce148d`**: 193 cases, 187 passing and 6 unsupported for
  named capabilities, the same set as after `085857e`.
- **Mutants**: every mutant of the fixes is killed. The fixture's ran 12 of 12 after the
  last fix, with two ported to its changed lines; there are also O1's, the name rule's
  and the closed link's.
