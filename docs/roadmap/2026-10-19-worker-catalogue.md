# Aliases are main's catalogue of workers

October 19, 2026, step 2 of [the worker profile](../v2/worker-profile.md). **Done.** An alias
is no launch: `rewake <alias>` is refused naming `rewake space open <space> --alias <alias>`.
Its `rewake` list became `role = "write" | "general"` (general when absent), and `main = true`
and `launch.apply` went; model and effort always apply, context where the harness has a flag. A
file with an unknown or removed field is refused whole, naming each field, its line and what
replaced it. `rewake workers` lost `--all` and `main`; a project alias still sets no role or
command. **Open.** The owner rewrites his `aliases.toml` with main; context on Claude Code comes
with step 1; step 3, the launch surface.
