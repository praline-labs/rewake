# The terminal emulator removed

October 18, 2026, by main's decision on the owner's of that day ([its entry](2026-10-18-decision.md)):
with a space shown only through `rewake attach`, `internal/host/terminal`'s emulator had no user.
It went with its tests and golden screen; the package keeps the pseudo-terminal pair, the window
size and raw mode, all on the standard library. `charmbracelet/x/vt` and the sixteen modules it
brought in left `go.mod`, which now requires `go-toml` alone, and the `depguard` rule that
confined the library went with them. The binary built with `-trimpath` shrank from 13 368 901 to
13 027 064 bytes.
