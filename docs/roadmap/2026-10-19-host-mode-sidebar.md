# The host mode: the sidebar

October 19, 2026, step 3 of [the host mode](../v2/host-mode.md): from 120 columns a sidebar of 28
right of the region lists main and each worker — its name, harness, model, effort, its turn or
state, its context — refreshed by the poll of the spaces; the current entry inverted, an ended one
dim; Ctrl+\ s hides or shows it, resizing every program; a click on an entry shows its tab; the
bar's hint names `s` where the sidebar can be shown. Checked live on Claude Code 2.1.302.
Open: step 4 (events and colors), the review, acceptance.
