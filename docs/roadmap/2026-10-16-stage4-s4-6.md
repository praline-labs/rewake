# Stage 4 of 2.0: a Claude Code worker's turns through the mod

Step S4-6 of the first working space
([the steps](../v2/stage4-space-steps.md#the-build-steps)), designed and built October 16, 2026
on branch `s4-6` from `v2` at `917c6fe`: the design and its readiness check on the Codex side,
then part A, the turns, part B, the heartbeat, and part C, the live acceptance, with a fix of the
notice's preview found live between A and B. Each part was reviewed on the Codex side: A was
accepted after its outbox fixes `3a7f823` and `069d9fd`, B in one round without a blocker. The
five checks ran green on `74e1ab6`; part C passed live on Claude Code 2.1.296 (below). A Claude
Code session of a reporting role launches and takes tasks again, its turns' ends reported by
its mod.

## What it built

- **The design** (`41fad53`, fixed in `2525fef`, `366d902`;
  [stage4-turns.md](../v2/stage4-turns.md), [its build](../v2/stage4-turns-build.md)). The mod
  reports the session's own turns without holding them: `TurnBoundary{Reports: true, HoldsEnd:
  false, AfterStart: true}`, live once the run's mod says hello. An end is retried under its id
  until its receipt and taken once, as its first capture; the start time is unknown and recorded
  as none; a heartbeat whose lapse withdraws what the mod proved; a boundary proven after the
  harness starts; the known causes that refuse a reporting role; the limits of not holding. The
  owner's and main's decisions of the day are its section "Decided".
- **Part A, the turns** (`d62a12b`, `b5f1005`, `192aea0`, `bc45ba1`, `0d71c33`, `9987fbe`;
  fixes `3a7f823`, `069d9fd`). A run's ownership of its name read three ways, so an end of a run
  whose name passed on is gone with no receipt and one whose record cannot be read is retried.
  The boundary proven after the start: not awaited before the harness starts, a known cause
  refusing the launch, availability waiting for it up to `AfterStartBound`. `turn/start`,
  `turn/end` and `mod/ended` in `modhost`, each end's first capture reused by every retry; the
  mod's `turn.start` and `turn.complete` for the main loop only, its outbox in `$.store` kept
  until a receipt or a run proven ended, a long answer cut at 1 MiB.
- **The notice's preview** (`22e93c2`, `b47b20d`). Found by main's live run of part A: a
  worker did a short task from the notice's preview and never read the message, so nobody got a
  report. The owner chose, of three options, to keep the preview and make it say it is only one:
  a task's or a question's preview ends by saying so, and the worker's playbook says a preview
  is never the task ([the record](../research-claude-mod.md#a-worker-acts-on-the-notices-preview)).
- **Part B, the heartbeat** (`e403f92`, `515fb71`, `74e1ab6`). The host binds a load's hello
  by its load number: a retry keeps its live generation, a later number or a lapsed load opens a
  new one, an earlier number or a superseded load is refused 410 naming the current load's
  number. `mod/alive` renews the generation on arrival and holds its answer up to 15 s with no
  lock; a lapse watch of 30 s, re-armed by every poll, withdraws TurnBoundary, and main's Spaces,
  leaving Wake. Every transition takes the next number of one sequence per run, and `modRun`
  drops a report not above the last, and every report after the close. The mod keeps one poll at
  a time for its newest generation on `$.clock.after`, says hello again on a 409, stops on a
  410, and after a hello's 410 takes the next number above the store's, its own and the
  refused one once. The rule clauses part A left as known limits name their tests.

## What the reviews found and how they were fixed

- **The design's readiness, three blockers.** B1: a successful no-op for a run that no longer
  owns its name was answered with a receipt the core never took, and an unreadable ownership
  read as not owned. Fixed: no receipt for a run proven gone, an unknown read retried. B3: a
  24-hour sweep could delete an unacknowledged end of a live run. Fixed: the outbox keeps an end
  until a receipt or its run is proven ended. B2: an old load's hello, outstanding across a
  reload, could become the newest generation and supersede the live load for good; ordering by
  arrival left it open at the re-check. Fixed by `366d902`: each load takes a number from a
  counter the run keeps in `$.store` before its first hello, and the host orders hellos by it.
- **Part A, two report-loss blockers**, each reproduced by the reviewer in a copy of the mod.
  An end added before a retried hello succeeded overwrote the older ends stored for the run; and
  an end stored by a load whose hello never arrived was refused 400 after a reload and dropped.
  Fixed in `3a7f823`: the run's stored ends are read before a load writes any, an unreadable
  backlog is not taken for empty, and each end is sent under the load that sends it. Re-checked
  and closed.
- **Part B:** accepted in one round, no blocker. Its notes: equal load numbers fall back to
  arrival order when the counter did not advance, a stated limit; the load numbers rest on the
  skill's sequential reload; the host test of the withdrawal reads the record, and the send's
  refusal is held by the CLI's live-set tests and by part C.

## Live

On Claude Code 2.1.296, on the cheapest model, each run in a private `REWAKE_DIR`
([the records](../research-claude-mod.md#s4-6-a-workers-turns-through-the-mod)):

- **Part A, by main:** a question from a print-mode main answered through the worker's
  `turn/end`, the boundary live 4 s after the start. Before the preview's fix the worker did the
  task from the preview and never read it; and a plain task from a print-mode main got no
  report, since main had exited first ([traps.md](../traps.md#a-task-sent-from-a-main-in-print-mode-claude--p-gets-no-report)).
- **Part C, by main and by the writer:** the report came back, and the boundary stayed live
  through 45 s idle between turns; a `--command` wrapper ending in `exec` admitted and its report
  back, one running Claude Code as its child never admitted and its task refused, exit 1; a mod
  that stopped polling lost its boundary 29 s after the reload, a task to it refused, the
  boundary back at once when the module was restored; a reload of the unchanged module kept it
  live; Escape typed mid-turn read by main as `stopped`; a subagent's turn not reported, one
  report per task; the hello 1.6–3.3 s after the harness started.

`AfterStartBound` stays 30 s, by main's decision of October 16, 2026
([readiness](../v2/stage4-turns.md#readiness-and-admission)).

## What stays open

- Whether the engine runs `turn.complete`'s hooks before the next turn starts: not observable
  from outside, and not observed.
- The limits of not holding an end ([stage4-turns.md](../v2/stage4-turns.md#what-not-holding-costs)):
  no start time, O1's confirmation, `classic.Stop` with `block` later.
- Equal load numbers after a store that refused the counter's write order by arrival.
- The causes that disable mods which rewake does not read yet — flags, settings keys, a
  person's own plugin named `rewake` — start a run whose hello never comes.
- `docs/rules/effects.md`, `docs/delivery.md` and `docs/traps.md` past 400 lines, and the slow
  plan test of `core/mail` ([work-queue.md](../archive/work-queue.md#also-queued-not-scheduled)).
- `rewake space open` and the spaces' side of the protocol are S4-7's.
