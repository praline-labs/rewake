# The workers' pane: its prefix, size and colors

October 18, 2026, from the owner's live look: leaving the pane took two Escapes, the second one
main's turn's. The key surface's prefix now carries the pane's commands — Alt+a then `q` closes
the pane, a digit chooses a worker, `z` switches the size. The pane opens full height and, docked,
about 104 columns, at most half the terminal; the worker is never sized below 100 by 30 and is
shown cropped instead. Off fullscreen the pane takes a line at a time with buttons. Dim and bold
are drawn as darker and brighter colors. Checked through a pseudo-terminal
([research](../research-claude-mod.md#the-panes-prefix-size-and-colors-later-the-same-day-21302)).
Open: a dock width the person kept wins over the pane's sizes; not yet accepted.
