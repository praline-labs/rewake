October 18, 2026, from the owner's live look: leaving the pane took two Escapes, the second one
main's turn's. The key surface's prefix now carries the pane's commands — Alt+a then `q` closes
the pane, a digit chooses a worker, `z` switches between the full and the compact size — and the
pane opens as large as the surface spares, the worker never sized below 100 by 30 and shown
cropped instead. Dim and bold are drawn as darker and brighter colors. Checked through a
pseudo-terminal ([research](../research-claude-mod.md#the-panes-prefix-size-and-colors-later-the-same-day-21302)).
