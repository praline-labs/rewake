# The shell, step 1: main and one worker in two panes

October 19, 2026 ([the design](../v2/shell.md)). The space socket's raw mode `b`: a preamble from
the grid's emulator — screen, pen, cursor, title, every input mode, the kitty stack as pushes, the
unfinished sequence's bytes — cut in `Grid.commit`; socket paths in `space list --json`; `rewake
--shell` execs `shell/` (Solid, run with `bun`), which runs main on its own pty, workers from their
hosts, the split, the bar, the prefix, the scanner and the palette. OpenTUI's signal handlers kept
the spike alive; the shell follows its terminal and ends main. Live on 2.1.302 and pi 1.2.0:
Shift+Enter in both panes, a worker shown, a resize across 120, a killed terminal leaving nothing.
Open: the owner's own terminal, steps 2–4, review.
