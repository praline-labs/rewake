# Stage 3 of 2.0: S15 accepted

S15 is the fifteenth step of stage 3 ([stage3.md](../v2/stage3.md)): the delivery server
to `host/delivery` ([stage3-moves.md](../v2/stage3-moves.md), the places from
[stage3-packages.md](../v2/stage3-packages.md)). It was built on October 11, 2026, on
branch `s3-s15` from `27cc2b5`: a record of the decided cut, preparatory commits in place,
the move, and this entry. It was reviewed and accepted on the Codex side the same day, after
one documentation fix.

## The cut

The inventory found the server's files and `core/mail`'s record code tangled both ways:
seven `(*Server)` methods sat in mail's files, and the bodies of the finished sweep, the
settlement and the awaiting sweep reached mail's private stop and copy handling. The cut
decided before the build: those three bodies stay in `core/mail` as locked entry points
(`SweepFinishedLocked`, `SettleLocked`, `SweepAwaitingLocked`), each called with the
mailbox lock already held; the server keeps the lock, the live-run check and the order
around them; a layout test holds the callers of the three to the server methods that
take the lock; and tests that need to fail a waiting copy's removal or watch the stop
listing do it through two operations of `core/mail` built only under `rewakefault`.
`9593ae9` records the cut in the plan before the code that relies on it.

## The review

An independent review read `27cc2b5..s3-s15` at `70dbf24` against the decided cut, not
only against the build's report: the production split, the lock discipline, the recovery
behaviour and the relocation of the tests, with a probe of the locked entry points'
callers and the four-variant dependency check run for it. It found no defect in the code
and no rule that lost its witness, and two findings:

- **R1, blocking, fixed in this round.** The destination map
  ([stage3-packages.md](../v2/stage3-packages.md#mail-inbox)) still sent the finished
  sweep's body, `availability` and `thread` to `host/delivery`: the cut that was turned
  down. Its rows now follow the decision — the server's methods, among them the sweep's
  wrapper with the lock and `liveRun`, to delivery; the bodies of the finished sweep, the
  settlement and the awaiting sweep to mail under their locked names; `availability` and
  `thread` kept in mail, less the thread writer `recordDeliveryThread`, which went to
  delivery; the lifetime record and the retention paths moved to mail. The paragraph of
  S13 in [stage3-moves.md](../v2/stage3-moves.md) on the file group says the two files
  left it.
- **N1, not blocking, older than the step.** Three removal-fault tests in
  `host/delivery` — `outcome_removal_test.go:65`, `watch_removal_test.go:44`,
  `report_removal_test.go:26` — do not check on their own that the waiting copy is still
  there: a fault that counts the attempt but removes the file passes all three. The same
  holds on `27cc2b5`, and the move kept their assertions as they were.
